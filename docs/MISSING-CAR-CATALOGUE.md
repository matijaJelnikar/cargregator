# Missing car catalogue backlog

Snapshot: 2026-11-03. Generated by `scripts/carinfo/coverage-audit.ts` from a read-only production snapshot and `coverage-candidates.json`.

The catalogue contains **822 generations across 421 models**. The candidate list identifies **168 absent entries**: 133 generation candidates and 35 model candidates.

Scope: an initial backlog of European cars sold during or after 2000, prioritising older gaps. This is not a complete inventory of every European car or new model launch. Rows marked as sourced have reviewed local research artifacts; other labels and dates remain research leads, not verified import data. A generation is matched by an explicit model/slug or alias, never just by overlapping dates. Held-back reports are present generations and must not be reinserted as missing cars.

The likely systemic cause is the 2011–2025 EEA registration input: `skeleton-prompt.ts` explicitly groups only those supplied variants. `knowledge.ts` fills models without prompts, but does not backfill older generations of models already represented by EEA rows.
