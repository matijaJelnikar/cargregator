## Why
The model page makes buyers dig through generation accordions to find engine advice and ownership risks.
A generation-wide verdict reflects only the best engine and can mislead buyers considering another variant.

## What Changes
- One selected generation drives the entire guide, with name and years always visible.
- Show a two-sentence excerpt from the existing report; keep the remainder expandable.
- Keep plain pros/cons lists and the primary car-search action.
- Show up to two good engine choices and one cautionary choice, with exact power, years and the existing explanation.
- Open the three priority risks by default, followed by the inspection checklist.
- Keep all engine variants, remaining problems, recalls and sources accessible below.
- Remove the generation-level verdict and repeated generation report sections.

Out of scope: regenerating reports, new factual claims, schema changes, Supabase writes, admin changes and deployment.
